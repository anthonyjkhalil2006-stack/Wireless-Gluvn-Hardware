GLUVN

A wireless wearable musical instrument

GLUVN turns finger movement, pressure and hand orientation into real-time musical control.

The Hardware

Each glove uses:

  • 5 flex sensors
  • 5 force-sensitive resistors (FSRs)
  • MPU-6886 IMU
  • M5StickC Plus 1.1 / ESP32
  • Bluetooth Low Energy

A CD4067 analog multiplexer allows the ten analog sensors to share a single ADC input.
